The problem: error accumulation in long exp() chains

Two mathematically identical ways to evaluate a chain of length N:

compose — product

p ← ∏ exp(xᵢ)

Per-step error is relative (~1 ulp, random sign) → independent & unbiased.

Random walk: error ~ ε·√N  (α ≈ 0.5)

expsum — sum then exp

s ← Σ xᵢ ,  exp(s)

Same-sign inputs make |s| grow linearly → late steps add larger absolute error.

Super-linear: error ~ ε·N1.5  (α ≈ 1.5)

Relative error scales as ε · Nα — so the α gap (0.5 vs 1.5) is a 100× hit at N = 10⁴ and 1000× at N = 10⁶.

Workload: all-negative xᵢ ∈ [-1e-2, -1e-4], evaluated in float, double, and fp32mp2 (software double-float on FP32).
